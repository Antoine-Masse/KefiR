1) Plan recognised: one factor, traitement, with 3 groups (temoin, froid,
   chaud); N = 90.
	==> Categorical response, 2 categories: non, oui.
	--> One question: do the proportions differ according to traitement?
	    (counterpart of the chi-squared test)
2) Checks on the data - exact computation, no minimum count.
	==> temoin: 18 oui, 12 non (of 30).
	==> froid: 18 oui, 12 non (of 30).
	==> chaud: 24 oui, 6 non (of 30).
	Note: small counts do not make the answer wrong, only less decisive
	      (unlike the chi-squared).
3) Law of the data - binomial.
	==> Each group has its own proportions of the 2 categories.
	==> No difference: the same proportions in every group.
	Note: no shape of the noise to compare with counts.
4) Starting point (priors) - 50.0 % chance of a difference.
	==> In each group, every set of proportions equally likely in advance.
	Note: these values are not tuned on the data; step 5 varies them.
5) Solidity of the answer [contingencyTableBF() {BayesFactor}] - Bayes factor
   0.463, the order can reverse.
	==> Bayes factor: 0.463, i.e. 2.16 in favour of no difference.
	==> Precision: exact computation, without draws.
	==> Sensitivity: the leading answer goes from 37.6 % to 81.2 %.
	--> Caution added: the answer can reverse; it will be called not
	    settled.
	Note: the Bayes factor says how many times more probable the counts are
	      with a difference than without.
	Note: what varies is the expected spread of the proportions and the
	      starting chances (1 against 2 to 2 against 1).
6) Which groups differ, and by how much [Dirichlet-multinomial {KefiR}, hdi()
   {bayestestR}] - temoin = froid < chaud ahead (54.3 %).
	==> If a difference exists, leading groupings: temoin = froid < chaud
	    (54.3 %); temoin < froid < chaud (16.6 %); froid < temoin = chaud
	    (14.5 %).
	==> temoin/froid: different at 18.8 %; froid - temoin = 0.2 points
	    [-23.6; 22.1].
	==> temoin/chaud: different at 35.1 %; chaud - temoin = 18.5 points
	    [-2.5; 40.3].
	==> froid/chaud: different at 35.1 %; chaud - froid = 19.0 points
	    [-3.7; 40.2].
	Note: gap of the share of oui, in points; [ ; ] = 95 % credible
	      interval.
	Note: all pairs are judged together, without a Tukey-like correction,
	      and cannot contradict each other.

   traitement: 68.4 %).
	==> Verdict: undecided.
	==> Probability of each hypothesis:
	    1. The proportions of germe do not differ according to traitement :

	    2. The proportions of germe differ according to traitement : 31.6 %

	Note: the data are 2.16 times more probable under hypothesis 1 than

	==> Not settled: temoin/chaud, froid/chaud.

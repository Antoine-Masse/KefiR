3) Shape of the noise.

	--> These values are not fitted on the data; step 5 varies them to see whether the answer holds.

	==> Sensitivity: r from 0.25 to 1, crossed with starting chances of a difference from 1 against 2 to 2 against 1. [m.bay() {KefiR}]
